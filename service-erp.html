// Subscription Platform

CEN: Subscription
Management System

CEN is our proprietary SaaS platform built for businesses that sell subscriptions, memberships, or recurring services. Full control over your revenue model — from plan creation to automated billing and user access management.

📋
Configurable PlansCreate unlimited subscription tiers with custom pricing, billing cycles, and feature access.
💳
Automatic BillingRecurring charge management, failed payment retries, and prorated calculations.

User & Member ControlRole-based access, membership status tracking, and user lifecycle management.

Renewal AlertsAutomated expiration alerts sent to users via email or WhatsApp before renewal.
