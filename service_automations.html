// Meta Business API

Chatbots on every
Meta channel.

We build chatbots using the official Meta Business API, connecting your business to where your customers already spend their time — WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook Messenger. With or without AI, we make your channels work for you 24/7.

// meta-api.js — webhook handler

app.post('/webhook', (req, res) => {
  const msg = req.body.entry[0]
    .changes[0].value.messages[0];

  if (msg.type === 'text') {
    const reply = bot.respond(msg.text.body);
    sendMessage(msg.from, reply);
  }

  res.sendStatus(200);
});

// Choose your approach

Chatbots with AI
or without AI — both work.

You don't need AI to build a powerful chatbot. Both approaches solve real problems — the right choice depends on your volume, budget, and complexity.

Without AI

Rule-Based Chatbots

Structured flows with predefined menus, keyword triggers, and configured automatic responses.

  • Predefined conversation trees and menus
  • Keyword and button trigger responses
  • FAQ automation with exact matching
  • Instant responses, zero training needed
  • Predictable behavior — always responds the same way
  • Lower investment, fast deployment

Best for

Structured, repetitive use cases

With AI

AI-Powered Chatbots

Conversational AI with natural language understanding that handles varied, complex customer interactions dynamically.

  • Natural language understanding (NLP)
  • Dynamic responses based on context
  • Handles topics not explicitly programmed
  • Learns from your business knowledge base
  • Manages complex, multi-step conversations
  • Scales without additional configuration

Best for

High-volume, varied conversations
